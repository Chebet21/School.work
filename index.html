My first pet

My first pet was a dog named Max. He was a chow chow and he loved to play fetch. Max was very friendly and loved to be around people.


My fluffiest pet

My fluffiest pet is a cat named Whiskers. She is a Maine Coon cat. She has soft, fluffy fur and loves to nap in sunny spots around the house.


My happiest pet

My happiest pet is a rabbit named Bugs. He is a Holland Lop rabbit. He has a cute, twitching nose and loves to hop around the garden.


My longest pet

My longest pet is a snake named Slither. He is a ball python. He has smooth, scales and loves to sunbathe on his basking spot.
